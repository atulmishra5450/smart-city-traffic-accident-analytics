Phase 6 — Key Business Insights

Insight 1 — Traffic-Congestion Correlation is Very Strong (r=0.81)
Vehicle count explains 65% of congestion variance. Reducing peak-hour vehicle load by 20% would directly reduce congestion. This justifies staggered working hours and demand-side policies.
Insight 2 — Peak Hour (08:00) Has Highest Traffic Load
Hour 8 averages 1232 vehicles — the highest of any hour. Morning rush hour (7–10 AM) and evening rush (5–8 PM) account for 82,392 records (33.3% of all readings).
Insight 3 — Average Emergency Response is 18.15 min (Above 15-min Target)
All 10 cities average above the 15-minute urban EMS benchmark. Pre-positioning ambulances at high-risk-score locations during peak hours is the single most impactful intervention available to city authorities.
Insight 4 — 2,982 Locations Have Very High Risk Score (≥6/10)
These 2,982 readings combine high congestion + poor visibility/road/signal/weather simultaneously. They are the top-priority locations for preventive infrastructure investment.
Insight 5 — Fatal Accidents are 5.96% of All Accidents
9,820 fatal accidents out of 164,749 total. Fatal + Severe together = 43,029 incidents (26.1%) requiring intensive emergency response.
Insight 6 — Kanpur has Highest Accident Rate (67.03%)
While all cities have similar accident rates (~66–67%), Kanpur leads consistently and should receive priority safety investment.
Insight 7 — Outer Ring Road is Most Congested (40.74% avg)
Among all 8 road types, Outer Ring Road has the highest average congestion. Signal coordination and bus rapid transit lanes on ring roads would yield city-wide benefit.
Insight 8 — Peak Hours Show Statistically Higher Accident Severity (p=0.046)
Welch t-test confirms peak-hour accidents are statistically more severe than off-peak (mean severity score: 1.2739 vs 1.2639). Enhanced monitoring and police presence during rush hours is data-justified.

Phase 7 — Business Recommendations

REC 1 — Ambulance Pre-Positioning: Deploy ambulances at the 2,982 identified Very High Risk locations during 07:00–10:00 and 17:00–20:00 to reduce the 18.15-minute average response time toward the 15-minute target.
REC 2 — Adaptive Signal Timing on Outer Ring Road: Deploy adaptive traffic signal control on Outer Ring Road (most congested at 40.74%). Estimated 10–15% congestion reduction based on comparable city implementations.
REC 3 — Real-Time Risk Dashboard: Use Traffic_Risk_Score ≥6 as an automated alert threshold. 2,982 such readings exist in this dataset — a real-time monitoring system would flag these before accidents occur.
REC 4 — Peak Hour Traffic Management: Hour 8:00 averages 1232 vehicles per location. Stagger government and school start times by 30 minutes to distribute this load across hours 7–10.
REC 5 — Emergency Corridor Designation: Designate dedicated emergency vehicle corridors on NH-27, MG Road, and Outer Ring Road (highest traffic + accident volumes) with signal pre-emption systems.
REC 6 — Kanpur Priority Safety Investment: Kanpur has the highest accident count (16,696) and rate (67.03%). Prioritise road safety audits and infrastructure upgrades in Kanpur for 2025–26.
REC 7 — Variable Message Signs: Deploy VMS on major expressways showing real-time congestion and risk alerts. Particularly important during fog events (fog present in dataset, low visibility flag triggered for 3-km readings).
REC 8 — Data Pipeline Investment: The real-time pipeline (app/pipeline.py) can ingest OpenWeatherMap + TomTom data every 15 minutes. Budget for API subscriptions and SQL Server auto-refresh to keep the Power BI dashboard live.
